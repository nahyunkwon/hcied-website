Set the Z height

Focus the Polar 50W on the top of your material with the stock 2-inch lens.

Jog the laser head

  1. Open the Control Panel from the toolbar or the View menu.
  2. Press Home Z and confirm the Z readout is 0.00 mm.
  3. Enter the calculated Jog Distance and press Return.
  4. Press Z Down once and check the Z readout.

Use Jog Distance and Z Down to set Z. With this Ruida RPA profile, Move to Position controls X and Y only.
