CSCE 743

Digital Fabrication Studio

Programmable materials, machine mechanisms, and end-user fabrication

Methods to operate and design new mechanisms for digital fabrication — 3D printing, laser cutting, CNC milling. Principles, models, theories, and prediction of behaviors in programmable materials, with emphasis on practical applications and late-breaking computational fabrication research in HCI for end-users.

The studio culminates in the formulation of a novel interaction technique for fabrication, grounded in mathematical representation of machine mechanisms and material behaviors.

15-week semester Studio format Grad classification required 3D Printing · Laser Cutting · CNC
Course Prerequisites
  • Hold graduate classification and secure instructor approval before enrolling.
  • Demonstrate proficiency in programming going into the studio.
  • Bring working knowledge of WebGL for material and mechanism visualization.
  • Manage an open-source project, including version control and collaborative workflows.
  • Prefer JavaScript and Node.js — useful, though not strictly required.
