HCIed Lab · Texas A&M University

Jeeeun Kim

My research identifies and resolves mismatches between human capabilities and the affordances of the physical world.

Associate Professor, Computer Science & Engineering
Director of the HCIed Lab · Faculty affiliate of TAMIDS · Founding organizer of TxHCI Seminar Series · Faculty advisor of Aggie Women in Computer Science (AWICS).

My research explores how people can adapt the physical world to their diverse abilities, tasks, and contexts. I develop end-user tools that transform passive everyday objects into adaptive interfaces capable of sensing, actuation, and context-aware behavior. My work combines AI for understanding human–object interactions with computational design and digital fabrication, enabling people to redesign and personalize the physical world. I also develop human-centric datasets that advance AI for understanding how people physically interact with objects.

My work has been recognized with the NSF CAREER Award, the Ralph E. Powe Junior Faculty Award, Montague-Center for Teaching Excellence Scholar, the Adobe Fellowship, and the TEES Young Faculty Fellow at Texas A&M University.
